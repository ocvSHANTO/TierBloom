Three simple steps. That’s it.

No account, no learning curve. Open the app and everything explains itself.

1

Add your photos

Pick as many photos as you like from your gallery, or create text cards with just a name. Your photos stay on your device — nothing is ever uploaded.

2

Arrange the tiers

Press and hold a card to drag it into place, or tap to select it, then tap a tier. Tier names, colours and order can all be changed.

Tap the big Export button at the bottom and choose a theme — the entire design transforms. Watch the live preview, then download as PNG or JPG.

A small tool with everything that matters

Everything a great tier list maker should have, built with care.

Touch designed for mobile

Cards lift after a 250 ms press-and-hold, so scrolling never turns into an accidental drag. Prefer tapping? Select a card, then tap a tier — use whichever feels easier.

Fully custom tiers

Tap a tier name to edit it: rename it, pick a colour, move it up or down. Colour options appear only in edit mode, so the board stays clean the rest of the time.

Save your board for later

Not finished yet? Save the board as a .tierbloom file and reopen it later — every photo and tier comes back exactly as you left it.

Private by design

There is no server and no tracking. Your photos never leave your browser, and the whole tool works offline.

Light and fast

A single HTML file with no heavy libraries and no external fonts. It runs smoothly on budget phones and loads in a blink.

Sharp exports at any size

From 1080px up to 4K, with the exact pixel size shown before you download. If a phone cannot render the largest size, it steps down one level and tells you.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere? +

Never. Your photos stay in your browser’s memory. TierBloom has no server, no accounts and no tracking.

How different are the themes, really? +

Each theme is drawn by its own renderer with its own layout, background, frames, typography and decoration. The Football theme turns your board into a stadium pitch, Polaroid pins taped frames to a wall, and the Newspaper theme prints your photos in black and white.

Why does the page scroll when I try to drag on my phone? +

Press and hold the card for a moment — once it lifts, drag it. This is deliberate, so scrolling and dragging never get mixed up. You can also tap a card to select it, then tap a tier to place it.

Can I finish a board later? +

Yes. Tap Save and the board downloads as a .tierbloom file. Open that file later with the Open button and everything is restored.

What is the largest export size? +

Up to 4K (3840px). On very old phones with smaller browser canvas limits, the size steps down automatically — and you are always told when that happens.
