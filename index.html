FAQ

Good questions.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Images are read and resized by your browser and never leave your device. Closing the page clears them.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-up, no email. Open the app and start ranking.

How does dragging work on a phone?

Press and hold an image for a moment until it lifts, then drag it into a tier. A normal swipe scrolls the page. Drop an image on the red bar at the top to remove it.

Why is my 4K or Ultra export smaller than expected?

Browsers limit canvas size, especially on phones. TierBloom shows the exact output size and reduces it automatically only when your device cannot render more.

Can I use exported images commercially?

Yes, as long as you own or are licensed to use the images you imported. See the Terms and Copyright pages in the footer.

Is TierBloom free?

Yes. If it helps you, the support button keeps an independent developer going.

Enjoying TierBloom?
