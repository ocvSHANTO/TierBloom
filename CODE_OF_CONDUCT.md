# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want TierBloom to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level or nationality.

## Expected behaviour
- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Credit others for their work

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, doxxing or sharing private information
- Sexualised content or attention
- Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Anything illegal or that you would not say to a colleague

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

Email **Junayed.I.Shanto@gmail.com** with details and links. Reports are handled privately and fairly.
